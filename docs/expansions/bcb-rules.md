# Bridges, Castles & Bazaars (Expansion 8): rules spec, 3rd edition

This file covers the rules of the expansion itself: bridges, castles and bazaars. The tile list and the interactions with the other expansions are researched separately. Interaction facts I came across are collected in §10 for that researcher.

**Edition implemented.** The current rulebook *Brücken, Burgen und Basare* (Hans im Glück, © 2010, 2017; PDF made Feb 2018) and its English translation *Bridges, Castles and Bazaars* (Z-Man / HiG, © 2010, 2018). It has the new artwork, and one of its tiles shows a garden. Our project calls this the "3rd edition". WikiCarpedia calls this tile design "C2" and the 2010 original "C1". The 2025 redesign *Castles & Bridges* is a **different expansion** and is **not** implemented (§11.4).

**Source labels** used on every claim:

* **RB**: rulebook. **RB-DE3** = 3rd-edition German, **RB-EN3** = 3rd-edition English, **RB-DE1** = 1st-edition German (2010), **RB-EN1** = Z-Man English of 2013 (1st-edition text), each with a page number. RB-DE3 is the original text. Where the English differs, RB-DE3 wins unless stated otherwise.
* **OC**: official clarification, with its date. These come from HiG or the publisher, as collected in S-CAR v7.3 or on WikiCarpedia.
* **COM**: community interpretation: WikiCarpedia editorial notes, S-CAR editor's notes, forum posts.
* **JCZ**: what JCloisterZone does.

## 1. Rulings at a glance

| Question | Ruling (3rd edition) | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Bridges and castles per player | 2–4 players: **3 bridges + 3 castles** each. 5–6 players: **2 + 2** each. | RB-DE3 p.1, RB-EN3 p.1 |
| Tiles | **12** land tiles, **8** of them with a bazaar | RB-DE3 p.1, RB-EN3 p.1 |
| When is a bridge built? | In step 1, right after you place the tile and **before** placing a follower. Optional. **1 per tile placement.** | RB-DE3 p.1 ("Nachdem du ein Plättchen angelegt hast, darfst du zusätzlich 1 Brücke bauen") |
| Where? | On the tile you just placed, **or** on a tile that shares a **side** with it (orthogonal only) | RB-DE3 p.1; RB-EN1 p.2 ("orthogonally adjacent") |
| Shape | Straight, from the middle of one edge to the middle of the opposite edge (N–S or W–E). Never diagonal. **Both ends on field edges.** It may span anything in between: a city, a monastery with a monk, a road, a garden, a river. At most **one bridge per tile**. | RB-DE3 p.1–2 (incl. figures); RB-EN3 p.1; RB-EN1 p.3 caption |
| Making a placement legal | A road may end against a field if a bridge carries it on **in the same turn**. The bridge goes on the new tile (new tile has field, neighbour has road) or on the neighbour (new tile has road, neighbour has field). Each bridge end must continue a road or face an **empty** square. All other edges must match normally. You may refuse the bridge and discard the tile instead. | RB-DE3 p.1; OC 12/2014; OC 10/2014 |
| Fields and cities under a bridge | **Not divided.** The bridge only adds a road. | RB-DE3 p.2; RB-EN3 p.2 |
| Follower on a bridge | Yes, as on a road (it must be unoccupied), **only** if the bridge is on the tile you just placed. It is your normal single follower placement. | RB-DE3 p.2; RB-EN3 p.2 |
| Bridge scoring | Like a road segment on that tile: 1 point per tile (2 with an inn). A tile counts **once** even if the road crosses it twice. | RB-DE3 p.2; OC 2/2014 |
| Removing a bridge | Never removed. Neutral, owned by nobody. | RB-DE3 p.1; Tower RB-EN3 lists "8. Bridge" as a neutral piece |
| Small city → castle | When a tile completes a city made of **exactly two semicircular segments**, the **owner** of that city (not the active player) chooses: score 4 points **or** cover it with one of his castles. The knight moves onto the castle. | RB-DE3 p.3; OC 7/2010 |
| Castle neighbourhood | **6 squares**: the 2 castle tiles and the 2 squares beside each castle tile on its long sides. In other words, a 2×3 block with the castle across its middle. Empty squares count too. | RB-DE3 p.3 + figure |
| Castle trigger | The **first** road, city, monastery or other castle completed **in a later turn** with at least one tile in the neighbourhood. For a monastery, its own tile must be in the neighbourhood. Gardens also trigger (COM/JCZ, see §9). Fields never trigger. It does not matter who completed the feature or whether anyone scored it. | RB-DE3 p.3–4; RB-DE1 p.4; S-CAR fn 368 |
| Castle points | The castle owner scores the **full** value of that feature, bonuses included. The castle meeple then goes home. The castle stays on the board and never scores again. Scoring is mandatory. | RB-DE3 p.3–4; OC 4/2014 |
| Several completions at once | The castle owner picks **one** of them. A castle that scores counts as a completed feature for neighbouring castles, and **all** of them score the same points. | RB-DE3 p.4 |
| Game end | Meeples still on castles are removed **before** final scoring and score 0. For farmers **every** castle is worth **4** (instead of 3). | RB-DE3 p.4 |
| Bazaar: when | After the complete normal turn (steps 1–3, scoring included) in which a bazaar tile was placed. | RB-DE3 p.4–5; S-CAR fn 345 |
| Bazaar: procedure | Reveal **one tile per player** (the active player gets one too). The player **left** of the active player picks a tile and opens the bidding (0 allowed). Each other player still without a tile then raises or passes **once**, clockwise. The picker **buys** (pays the high bid to the high bidder) or **sells** (the high bidder pays him and takes the tile). If only the picker bid, he pays his bid to the bank. Anyone with a tile drops out, and the last tile is free. Scores may go **negative**. | RB-DE3 p.5; RB-EN3 p.5 |
| After the auction | Everyone plays their tile as a full normal turn, starting **left of the bazaar player** and ending with him. Nobody draws in this round, and play then goes on normally from the left player. Bazaars on bought tiles don't trigger. | RB-DE3 p.5; RB-EN3 p.5 |
| Too few tiles | If fewer tiles are left in the stack than there are players, there is no auction. | RB-EN3 p.5 (not in RB-DE3); JCZ |

## 3. Components and setup

* **12 land tiles**, marked with the expansion symbol (an arch). **8 show a bazaar.** Two of the twelve tiles have an inn (I&C tie-in). (RB-DE3 p.1; WikiCarpedia tile list.) The tile layouts are the tile researcher's job. JCZ has tile set `bridges-castles-and-bazaars:2` for this edition: `BB/CFR!G` carries the garden; the 1st-edition `BB/CFR!` has none.
* **12 castle tokens** (cardboard: an oval patch of meadow with a small castle on it, big enough to cover two facing city caps) and **12 wooden bridges** (RB-DE3 p.1).
* **Per player** (RB-DE3 p.1: "Bei 2 bis 4 Spielern gibst du jedem (auch dir) 3 Brücken und 3 Burgen. Bei 5 und 6 Spielern gibst du jedem (auch dir) 2 Brücken und 2 Burgen."):

| Players | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Bridges each | 3 | 3 | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| Castles each | 3 | 3 | 3 | 2 | 2 |

  Spare pieces go back in the box. Each player's bridges and castles are a **personal supply**: when yours are gone, you cannot build any more.
* Shuffle the 12 tiles into the land tiles (RB-DE3 p.1).
* The whole expansion, or only parts of it, may be used (RB-DE3 p.1: "kann komplett oder teilweise ins Spiel integriert werden"). So **bridges, castles and bazaars can be separate options.** Without the bazaar module, a bazaar is just a picture.

## 4. Turn sequence: where each part hooks in

The step names follow `MASTER.md` §A.2.

```
Step 1  Draw        – during a bazaar round: play the tile you got at the bazaar; draw nothing
Step 2  Place tile  – 2a legality: ONE bridge may cure road↔field mismatches (mandatory bridge),
                      or the player may refuse and discard (OC 10/2014)
                    – 2a′ if no bridge was used: optional bridge on the new tile or an orthogonal
                      neighbour (RB-DE3 p.1)
                    – (then the other placement effects: builder trigger, tower, volcano – interactions)
Step 4  Follower    – the normal single choice; a figure may also go on a bridge on the NEW tile
Step 6  Scoring     – 6a each just-completed SMALL CITY: its owner scores 4 or builds a castle
                      (knight moves onto the castle)
                    – 6b score the completed features as usual (castle cities are NOT completed)
                    – 6c castle triggers: every occupied castle built in an EARLIER turn/part whose
                      6-square area holds part of a feature completed in 6b scores that value; the
                      meeple goes home; scored castles count as completed for other castles (chain)
Step 6d Bazaar      – if the tile placed in THIS part was a drawn bazaar tile, no bazaar round is
                      running, and the stack holds ≥ #players tiles: auction, then the bazaar round
                      (each player from the left of P round to P plays a full normal turn with the
                      tile they got)
Step 7  Builder     – the second part comes AFTER the bazaar round (RB-DE3 p.6); JCZ/CAR do it before
Step 8  End         – next regular turn: the player left of P
Game end            – remove the meeples on castles (0 points); farms: castle = 4
```

* **Bridge timing.** RB-DE3 puts the bridge in "1. Landschaftsplättchen legen" and the meeple in "2. Einen Meeple einsetzen", so the bridge comes first. S-CAR fn 360: "the bridge is generally built before deploying a follower." **JCZ**: building a bridge re-enters the action phase, so you can still place a meeple afterwards. Once a meeple is placed, no bridge is offered. Building a bridge after placing your follower is therefore **not** allowed in our implementation. The 1st-edition text gave no time ("In seinem Zug … zusätzlich zum Legen einer Karte und zum Setzen eines Gefolgsmannes 1 Brücke bauen", RB-DE1 p.2). S-CAR fn 356 and the multi-edition WikiCarpedia *Order of Play* read that text as "any time in the turn" (COM).
* **Castle decisions happen in the scoring step**, as the resolution of the small city, "instead of" scoring it (RB-DE3 p.3: "darf der Besitzer der Kleinstadt sich entscheiden, ob er sie normal wertet (4 Punkte) oder, ob er eine Burg darauf baut"). WikiCarpedia *Order of Play* step 3B: "the small city may be converted into a castle by the occupying player. If converted, this feature's completion is considered to be resolved." The relative order against other scorings never changes the result: a castle built now cannot score this turn, and a converted small city is not completed for older castles. **JCZ** asks all castle questions before scoring (`CastlePhase` comes before `ScoringPhase`), starting with the active player and going clockwise. S-CAR fn 365: "this is after the Move Wood phase and does not itself constitute a Move Wood phase (and might not even involve the active player). (5/2013)".
* **Castle scoring comes right after the feature that triggers it.** *Order of Play* COM summary of the RB rules: "A castle must be resolved immediately after any feature which triggers its completion … Completed castles overlapping other completed castles' fiefs must be resolved together."
* **Bazaar timing.** RB-DE3 p.4: "Ziehst du ein Plättchen mit Basar, führst du damit zuerst einen normalen Zug mit 1. … 2. … 3. … durch." Then p.5: "4. Ein Basar wird veranstaltet". RB-EN3 p.5: "After you have placed a bazaar and completed your turn, you must 4. host a bazaar auction." S-CAR fn 345: the RGG edition says "including placing a follower, scoring", so the auction comes "at the end of the turn, after scoring, not immediately after tile placement".

## 5. Bridges

### B1. When, and how many?

* **Rulebook.** RB-DE3 p.1: "Nachdem du ein Plättchen angelegt hast, darfst du **zusätzlich 1 Brücke** bauen." RB-EN3 p.1: "After placing a tile, you may place a bridge". It is optional: "may"/"darfst".
* It happens in step 1, after the tile is placed and before the follower (see §4).
* **How many:** one per tile placement. RB-DE3 ties it to "after placing a tile". RB-DE1 said "In seinem Zug … 1 Brücke" ("in his turn"). **JCZ** allows one bridge per **turn part** (`BridgeCapability` checks the bridge events of the current turn part), so a builder double turn may use two. Recommendation: **one per tile placement**, which also covers bazaar-round turns. See open question Q10.
* You need a bridge left in your supply (JCZ: no bridge options without a token).

### B2. Where?

* **Rulebook.** RB-DE3 p.1: "auf das gerade gelegte Plättchen … **oder** auf ein Plättchen, das **mit einer Seite** an das gerade gelegte Plättchen angrenzt". RB-EN3: "on a tile touching the tile you just placed". RB-EN1: "on a tile that is **orthogonally adjacent** to a newly placed tile". So: the new tile or one of its (up to) 4 orthogonal neighbours. Diagonal tiles never.
* An optional bridge on a neighbouring tile is allowed by the text even when it is not needed for the placement. S-CAR fn 356 (COM) imagines exactly this: "… opts to place a bridge on that tile (or even the adjacent one) such that the bridge ends are on tile edges that do not touch other tiles."
* **JCZ** offers an optional bridge only on the **new** tile. A bridge on a neighbour is offered only as a *mandatory* bridge that makes the placement legal (`PlacementOption.mandatoryBridge`). That is a JCZ simplification (open question Q7). Because of the end rule in B3, an optional bridge on an existing neighbour always has both ends facing empty squares, so it runs parallel to the shared edge.

### B3. Geometry

* **Straight, edge to edge.** RB-EN3 p.1: "it must be placed facing either left-to-right or up-to-down (it cannot be placed diagonally across the tile)". In JCZ the bridge locations are `Location.BRIDGES = [NS, WE]`.
* **Both ends on field edges.** RB-DE3 p.1: "Dabei müssen beiden Brückenpfeiler auf einer Wiese stehen (nicht auf Städten, Straßen, Flüssen etc.)". RB-EN3: "you must place both ends in a field". **JCZ** `EdgePattern.isBridgeAllowed`: for N–S, the tile's N and S edges must both be `FIELD`; for W–E, its W and E edges. The **printed** edge type of that tile counts, so a pure field edge qualifies and a city, road or river edge does not.
* **What it may span.** The ends are the only constraint. The middle may pass over:
  * a **city**: RB-DE3 p.2 figure 1, a bridge W–E over a N–S city band ("führst du die Straße mit einer Brücke über den Stadtteil hinweg");
  * an **occupied monastery**: RB-DE3 p.2 figure 4 ("über ein besetztes Kloster"); RB-DE3 p.1: "Du darfst eine Brücke auch über ein besetztes Plättchen bauen";
  * a **road** and a **garden**: RB-DE3 p.2 figure 4 (inspected at 600 dpi). The middle tile has a N–S road and a garden bed, and a W–E bridge spans both;
  * a **river**: RB-EN1 p.3 caption "Bridges may also be constructed one after the other over roads, monasteries, rivers, etc."
* **Bridges in a row** are fine: "Du darfst eine Brücke hinter eine andere Brücke bauen" (RB-DE3 p.1).
* **One bridge per tile.** RB-DE3 p.1: "Auf einem Plättchen darf nur 1 Brücke stehen." So no crossing bridges either (S-CAR fn 361). JCZ also rejects a tile that already has a bridge.
* **Which tiles can take a bridge, in practice:** tiles with two opposite field edges. That includes all-field tiles (monastery, garden, plain field, `BB/F.b` bazaar), **straight roads** (crossing at right angles), N–S city bands with field at W and E, two opposite caps (bridge between them), single caps (bridge parallel to the cap), straight river tiles, and so on. **Not possible:** curves, junctions, crossroads, corner cities, three-sided cities, or any tile without two opposite field edges.

### B4. Using a bridge to make a placement legal

* **Rulebook.** RB-DE3 p.1: "Du darfst das Plättchen auch so legen, dass eine Straße an einer Wiese endet, wenn du diese Straße im gleichen Zug mit einer Brücke weiterführst." RB-EN3: "You can place a tile so that a road ends against a field, but you must place a bridge that continues the road on that same turn."
* **Both directions are shown** in the RB-DE3 p.2 figures:
  * **Bridge on the new tile.** The new tile has a field edge where the neighbour's road arrives. Figure 1: Blue's road arrives from the west at the field edge of a new city-band tile, and the bridge W–E over the city carries it on.
  * **Bridge on the neighbouring tile.** The new tile's road runs into a field edge of an existing tile. Figure 2: you place a road tile, put a meeple on its road, and bridge the existing city-band tile next to it. The caption reads "Mit der Brücke führst du die Straße auf dem angrenzenden Plättchen fort."
  * **Illegal example** (figure 3): the existing tile's west edge is a road, so a W–E bridge would stand on a road. The new road tile therefore cannot go there. Caption: "So darfst du die Brücke nicht bauen, da ein Brückenpfeiler auf einer Straße stehen würde."
* **All edges must match once the bridge is in.** The only mismatch a bridge can cure is **road against field**, at the bridged edge(s). Each bridge end must also connect properly. OC 12/2014 (S-CAR fn 358, WikiCarpedia fn 2): "As with normal roads, each end of the bridge must continue a road segment (or bridge) already in play or must be adjacent to an empty place for a tile." Consequences:
  * A bridge **on the new tile** cures 1 or 2 mismatches (one at each end; e.g. a new tile dropped between two facing road ends). Its other end must face an empty square or a road. It may **not** face a neighbour's field or city edge.
  * A bridge **on a neighbouring tile T** cures only the edge between T and the new tile. Its **far end** must face an **empty** square: any tile already there matched T's field edge, so it shows field, and road against field is illegal.
  * City, river or double mismatches on different axes can never be cured. Only one bridge is allowed per placement.
* **JCZ** follows this exactly. `getTilePlacements` first tries the tile as printed, then with a bridge on the tile (N/S or W/E sides become `ROAD`, and the result must match the border exactly), then with a bridge on each placed neighbour (`isBridgePlacementAllowed`: no bridge there yet, both edges field, and every end either facing an empty square or a "city gate", a feature from another expansion). JCZ keeps only the first option per position and rotation. Our UI should offer every alternative (e.g. N–S on the tile vs. on the neighbour) where more than one exists.
* **You may refuse.** OC 10/2014 (S-CAR fn 357): "A player is not required to use a bridge to place a tile, even if that tile would otherwise have to be discarded. The player has the choice to use the bridge to place the tile or just discard the tile." **JCZ** `TilePhase`: the player may *pass* (discard and draw again) when every legal placement needs a bridge.

### B5. Fields and cities under a bridge

* **Rulebook.** RB-DE3 p.2: "Wiesen oder Städte unter einer Brücke sind nicht getrennt." RB-EN3 p.2: "Bridges do not separate cities or fields."
* A bridge **adds** a road feature. It removes nothing and splits nothing. A printed road on the same tile still separates fields as printed: a straight W–E road under a N–S bridge still has its north and south fields apart, and the bridge splits neither of them.
* **Fields on the neighbouring tile do join across its road** (derived from RB plus edge geometry; JCZ behaves this way because it never changes field features). The bridged tile's field still owns **both halves** of the bridged edge. The neighbouring tile's road arrives in the middle of that edge, and the fields on its two sides each touch one half. So **both fields beside the neighbour's road connect into the single field under the bridge.** Example (RB-DE3 p.2 figure 1): the fields north and south of Blue's road tile both join the west field of the bridged city tile.
* This can merge two large farms in one move, so the AI should know about it (§14).

### B6. Followers on bridges

* **Rulebook.** RB-DE3 p.2: "Hast du die Brücke also auf das **gerade gelegte** Plättchen gebaut, darfst du deinen Meeple (oder eine andere Figur) auch auf die Brücke einsetzen." RB-EN3: "… as if it were a road."
* **Only on a bridge on the new tile.** A bridge on a neighbouring tile cannot take a figure, because figures go only on the new tile (WikiCarpedia examples 6/7). Any bridge on the new tile was necessarily built this turn.
* It is the **normal one-follower placement**, under road rules: the whole road (with all its bridges) must be unoccupied (WikiCarpedia example 6: "The road with the bridge must be unoccupied."). Because the bridge comes first (§4), a bridge that joins your new road to an occupied road stops you from placing a meeple on that road this turn.
* Figures allowed on a bridge (RB-DE3 p.6): normal meeple, large meeple (I&C), builder (T&B; it needs your thief on that road as usual). Out of scope: wagon, ringmaster. The abbot cannot go on a bridge, since it only takes monasteries and gardens.

### B7. Scoring

* **Rulebook.** RB-DE3 p.2: "Die Brücke wird bei einer Wertung genauso gewertet wie eine Straße auf einem Landschaftsplättchen." RB-EN3: "Bridges are scored like tiles with a road on them." RB-DE1 p.3: "(die Brücke zählt 1 Punkt wie ein normales Straßenstück)". Figure (RB-DE3 p.2): a road of 6 tiles, 2 of them bridges, completes for **6 points**.
* **A tile counts once.** OC 2/2014 (S-CAR fn 359): "any completed road that includes a bridge and a printed road segment on the same tile still only scores 1 point for the tile." Our `getGroup` collects the tiles in a `Set`, so this already holds.
* **Inn (I&C) on a bridged tile.** The inn affects only the printed road beside it. A bridge gets the bonus only if it belongs to the same road as that printed segment (OC 1/2021, WikiCarpedia). The usual "inn road" rules then apply to the whole road.
* **Game end:** no special effect. An incomplete road with bridges scores 1 per tile (0 with an inn, per our I&C rules), as usual. Bridges stay on the board.

### B8. Removal and ownership

* **Never moved or removed.** RB-DE3 p.1: "Die Brücke bleibt das ganze Spiel über auf dem Plättchen stehen." RB-EN1 p.2: "The castles and the bridges remain in play until the end of the game."
* **Owned by nobody on the board.** The supply is personal, but a built bridge is just road; road majority decides scoring. The Tower 3rd-edition rulebook counts "8. Bridge" and "8. Castle" among "neutral figures and tokens".

## 6. Castles

### C1. Which cities qualify

* **Rulebook.** RB-DE3 p.3: "Wenn du eine Kleinstadt (das ist eine Stadt, die nur aus **zwei halbrunden Stadtteilen** besteht), abschließt, darf der Besitzer der Kleinstadt sich entscheiden, ob er sie normal wertet (4 Punkte) oder, ob er eine Burg darauf baut." RB-DE1 is the same ("bestehend aus nur zwei halbrunden Stadtteilen").
* **OC 7/2010** (Christof Tisch, HiG, BGG thread 538079, quoted by WikiCarpedia fn 6): only two **semicircular** segments qualify. "The castle token has to cover the small city completely". A triangular segment does not qualify.
* **The English differs.** RB-EN3 p.3: "a small city (a city consisting of only 2 tiles)". RB-EN1: "two city end segments". Taken literally these allow odd shapes. Our ruling follows RB-DE3 and the OC (open question Q6).
* **Geometry.** A completed city on exactly 2 tiles always consists of 2 one-edge segments meeting at the shared edge. The question is only about the **shape**. **JCZ** marks every one-edge city segment as castle-capable (`CASTLE_BASE`: one open edge, no multi-edge) unless the tile XML says `castle-base="false"`. The castle needs exactly 2 such segments.
* **Excluded segments in our tile sets** (JCZ `castle-base="false"`; WikiCarpedia lists the BCB and P&D ones as "witch-hat"/"top city segment" examples):
  * `BB/CFR!` / `BB/CFR!G`: BCB's own pointed/triangular city on one edge;
  * `IC/C!`: I&C wedge-shaped city (JCZ only);
  * `DG/CCc+.p`: P&D princess cap narrowing into a band down to the corner.

  All other one-edge caps qualify, including the big 3rd-edition caps that reach the tile centre (WikiCarpedia COM) and the Tower's `TO/RCr` (JCZ).
* **Pennants.** No one-edge city segment in our tile sets has a pennant (checked by script over all `docs/expansions/*.json`), so every small city is worth exactly 4. The rule text assumes this ("normal wertet (4 Punkte)"). JCZ does not test for pennants.
* **Only at the moment of completion.** It replaces the scoring of a small city completed this turn. A small city scored earlier can never become a castle (RB-DE3 p.3 example: "Die Kleinstadt auf 3 und 6 wurde schon vor dem Bau der Burg gewertet").
* **It must be occupied.** Without an owner nobody can build. An empty small city is simply completed and scored by nobody.
* It does not matter whether the knight was already on the first half or is placed on the second half this turn (RB-DE3 p.3: "Beim Burgenbau ist es egal, ob dein Meeple schon vor dem Bau auf der ersten Stadthälfte steht oder ob du den Meeple erst beim Abschließen der Stadt auf die zweite gerade gelegte Stadthälfte einsetzt.").

### C2. Who decides

* **The owner of the small city**, i.e. the player with the most knights in it, **not** the player who completed it. RB-DE3 p.3: "darf der Besitzer der Kleinstadt sich entscheiden". RB-EN3: "if a player has a meeple occupying that city, he or she chooses". RB-DE1: "kann der Besitzer der Kleinstadt sich entscheiden". JCZ: `CastlePhase` asks each player with an option, starting with the active player.
* **Several owners cannot happen in our scope.** A two-segment city can hold only one knight. When the second cap is placed, a knight may go on it only if the first cap was empty, and portal or tower placement also needs an unoccupied feature. A builder (T&B) may share the city with its owner's knight, but the builder is not a knight. (Only *The Flying Machines*, out of scope, lets several players into a small city; WikiCarpedia has rules for that case.) A large meeple is one knight. **JCZ** offers the castle only when the city holds **exactly one** follower.
* **No castle left: no choice.** The owner places "eines seiner Burgplättchen aus seinem Vorrat" (RB-DE3 p.3), so with none left he must score normally. JCZ offers no castle without a token.
* One tile may complete **two** small cities (e.g. a tile with two opposite caps). Each owner decides separately.

### C3. Timing of the decision

* In the **scoring step**, instead of scoring that small city (RB-DE3 p.3, under "3."). The castle is "not yet completed": "Eine Burg mit Meeple ist noch nicht abgeschlossen." (RB-DE3 p.3.) So:
  * other features completed by the same tile score normally;
  * a castle built now does **not** count as a completed feature for older castles. Only a small city that is *scored* is a completed city that can trigger them;
  * the new castle cannot itself be triggered this turn (C6).
* Implementation: ask first (as JCZ does), then score, then resolve the castle triggers.

### C4. The knight

* It moves **onto the castle** and stays there (RB-DE3 p.3: "… stellt den Meeple, der auf der Stadt stand, auf die Burg"). RB-EN1 calls it the "lord"; the HiG text keeps calling it a knight (S-CAR fn 366). It is no longer in a city and cannot be scored as a knight.
* **Large meeple allowed** (RB-DE3 p.6: "Auf die Burg darfst du folgende Figuren einsetzen: 1. Erw.: Großer Meeple"). Its strength does not matter afterwards: the castle scores the same points.
* While waiting it is safe from the dragon and from tower capture (RB-DE3 p.6). See §10.

### C5. The neighbourhood

* **Rulebook.** RB-DE3 p.3: "Die Umgebung besteht aus den 2 Plättchen, auf denen die Burg liegt, sowie den 2 Plättchen rechts und den 2 Plättchen links davon – insgesamt also 6 Plättchen." RB-EN3: "either one of the 2 tiles containing your castle, the 2 tiles to the right, or the 2 tiles to the left".
* **Precisely.** Let the castle lie on tiles A and B, with A north of B.

  ```
  W(A)  A  E(A)
  W(B)  B  E(B)
  ```

  The area is those **6 squares**. "Left/right" means across the castle's **long sides**, perpendicular to the A–B axis. Not included: the squares beyond the castle's ends (N of A, S of B) and the 4 squares diagonal to those ends. For a horizontal castle (A west of B) the area is A, B and the squares N and S of each. **JCZ** `Castle.getVicinity()` does exactly this. Empty squares belong to the area, and a tile placed there later counts.
* **Rulebook figure (RB-DE3 p.3, RB-EN3 p.3).** Two rows of three squares, numbered 1-2-3 on top and 4-5-6 below. The castle token, with a red meeple, lies across the edge between **2 and 5**: tile 2's southern cap and tile 5's northern cap made the small city. Square **4** is empty (dashed outline). Tile 1 shows a **monastery with a road** running south. Tile 3 has a city cap at the top, and at the bottom the cap of another small city (3–6) that was scored before the castle existed. Tile 6 has that small city's top cap and a city on its east side. Caption: the castle can score "das Kloster auf 1 · die Straße auf 1 · die Stadt auf 3 oben · die Stadt auf 6 rechts", but not the small city 3–6, which was already scored.

### C6. What triggers a castle

* **Feature types.** RB-DE3 p.3: "wenn das erste Bauwerk (**Kloster, Stadt oder Straße**) in der Umgebung deiner Burg **in einem späteren Zug** abgeschlossen wird". RB-DE1 p.4: "Erst wenn ein Bauwerk (Kloster, Straße, Stadt **oder eine andere Burg**), welches mindestens auf einer dieser 6 Karten vertreten ist, fertiggestellt wird". RB-DE3 p.4 on chains: "Bekommt eine Burg Punkte, zählt sie für die Burg daneben als abgeschlossenes Bauwerk."
  * road ✔, city ✔ (including a small city that is *scored*), monastery ✔, another castle that scores ✔;
  * **garden (Abbot)**: not mentioned in any rulebook. WikiCarpedia's trigger table (COM) includes it ("the garden tile overlaps the castle fief"), and in **JCZ** `Garden implements Monastic`, which is collected as a completed feature. Recommendation: **gardens trigger, like monasteries** (open question Q5);
  * **fields never** trigger. RB-DE1 p.4: "Es gibt keine Punkte für eine Wiese." RB-EN1: "A castle does not score points for a field in its fief." S-CAR: "A castle gets no points from farms in its vicinity."
  * Building a castle **never** triggers another castle, because it is not a completion.
* **What "in the area" means.** RB-DE3 p.3: "Es muss nur **ein Teil** des abgeschlossenen Bauwerkes auf einem oder mehreren Umgebungsplättchen vorhanden sein." Any tile of the road or city counts; a bridge tile counts as a road tile. The tile that completes the feature does not have to be in the area.
  * **Monastery:** only if the monastery tile itself is in the area. RB-DE3 p.4: "Ein Kloster liegt nur dann in der Umgebung, wenn das Kloster selbst auf einem der 6 Plätze liegt … Es reicht nicht aus, wenn eines der 8 Plättchen der Klosterumgebung in der Burgumgebung liegt." Gardens the same way (COM/JCZ).
  * **Castle as trigger:** a scored castle counts if one of its 2 tiles is in the other castle's area (COM WikiCarpedia fn 20; JCZ). See C6 chains and Q11.
* **Who completed it, and whether anyone scored it, does not matter.** RB-DE3 p.4: "Ob jemand Punkte für das nächste abgeschlossene Bauwerk bekommt, ist für die Burgwertung egal. Du erhältst für deine Burg auch Punkte für ein abgeschlossenes Bauwerk, für das niemand sonst Punkte bekommt." RB-DE1: "Die Burg bekommt auch Punkte für ein Bauwerk, auf dem kein Gefolgsmann steht." So **unoccupied** completed roads, cities, monasteries and gardens trigger, at their full value (OC 2/2015, WikiCarpedia fn 16: "points are tallied for all completed features, even unoccupied ones").
* **Mandatory, first completion only.** RB-DE3 p.4: "Du bekommst für deinen Meeple auf der Burg immer die Punkte für das **nächste** abgeschlossene Bauwerk. Du darfst den Meeple nicht stehen lassen, um für ein später abgeschlossenes Bauwerk Punkte zu bekommen."
* **Several completions in one turn.** RB-DE3 p.4: "Falls in einem Zug mehrere Bauwerke in der Umgebung einer nicht abgeschlossenen Burg fertiggestellt werden, entscheidet der **Besitzer der Burg**, für welches Bauwerk er Punkte bekommt. Eine Burg bekommt immer nur Punkte von einem Bauwerk." ("You" means the castle owner, not the active player; WikiCarpedia fn 17.) RB-EN3's "you decide what order they are scored. As always, you only score points for the first feature" is a mistranslation (WikiCarpedia fn 19). In practice the owner takes the **highest** value (WikiCarpedia fn 18). **JCZ** picks the maximum automatically. Nothing in our scope makes a lower value better, so automatic maximum is equivalent.
* **One completion, several castles.** Yes. Every waiting castle whose area contains part of the feature scores, each owner the full value. The rule is per castle, and JCZ loops over all occupied castles.
* **Chains.** RB-DE3 p.4: "Es können auch zwei oder mehrere nicht abgeschlossene Burgen nebeneinander stehen. Bekommt eine Burg Punkte, zählt sie für die Burg daneben als abgeschlossenes Bauwerk. **Alle Burgbesitzer bekommen gleich viele Punkte.**" Each chained castle scores the value of the **original** feature. **JCZ** `CastleCapability.scoreCastles`: first the direct triggers, then repeated passes in which every still-occupied castle whose area intersects the tiles of a castle scored this turn scores the same value, until nothing new scores.
  * The direction is not symmetric. Castle X triggers castle Y only if one of X's tiles lies in **Y's** area (COM WikiCarpedia fn 20, with an example; JCZ). RB-EN1 words it the other way round ("other castles in its fief"). Q11.
  * If a castle can be reached from several sources with different values, give it the **highest** (the owner chooses). WikiCarpedia fn 21 (COM) suggests the active player decides, and JCZ takes the first source found. Q12.
* **Same turn.** A castle **cannot** be triggered in the turn it is built: "in einem späteren Zug" (RB-DE3 p.3; RB-DE1 p.3; RB-EN1 "on a later turn"). RB-EN3 leaves this out. S-CAR fn 368: "a feature that is completed on the turn that the castle is built does not allow the castle to score."
  * **Builder double turn.** WikiCarpedia fn 13 (COM) reads "later turn" as "a later round of scoring". A castle built in part 1 can then be triggered in part 2. **JCZ** agrees: it excludes only castles that include the tile placed in the current part (`getOccupiedCastles`). Recommendation: follow that (Q4).
  * Turns in a bazaar round are separate turns, so a castle built in one of them can be triggered by a later one.

### C7. What the castle scores

* **The full value of the feature.** RB-DE3 p.3: "Der Besitzer des Bauwerkes und der Besitzer der Burg bekommen beide die **volle Punktzahl** des Bauwerkes." The value is computed as if the feature were scored for its owner, bonuses included. OC 4/2014 (S-CAR fn 370): "This is after any bonuses or penalties (such as the Cathedral or Witch) are accounted for." RB-DE3 p.6: inn and cathedral bonuses also count for the castle. Examples: monastery or garden 9; road 1 per tile (2 with an inn); city 2 per tile and pennant (3 with a cathedral); a scored small city 4.
* If you own both the castle and the feature, you score **twice** (RB-DE3 p.4).
* The castle meeple then **returns** to its owner (RB-DE3 p.3: "Dann wird der Meeple von der Burg zurückgenommen."). The token **stays** for the rest of the game ("Das Burgplättchen bleibt liegen."). It is now complete and **never scores again**. No figure can ever be placed on a castle: castles are not features of newly placed tiles.
* It is not a move-wood action and does not use the turn's follower step.

### C8. End of game

* **Waiting castles score nothing.** RB-DE3 p.4: "Entferne alle Meeple, die am Spielende noch auf einer Burg stehen, vor der Schlusswertung vom Spielfeld. Für sie bekommt niemand mehr Punkte." End-of-game scoring of incomplete features does not trigger castles. JCZ `FinalScoring` gives `castle.incomplete` 0.
* **Farmers.** RB-DE3 p.4: "Eine Burg auf der Wiese zählt für die Bauernwertung **4 Punkte** (statt 3 Punkte wie eine normale abgeschlossene Stadt)." RB-EN3: "castles are worth 4 points (instead of 3)."
  * This applies to **every** castle, triggered or not. The meeples are removed first, so they can't be told apart. S-CAR fn 375 calls the RGG summary "farms only score completed castles" a misprint. JCZ `Field.getCityPoints` counts every `Castle` at 3 + 1.
  * A castle counts **once** per field (it replaces the 2-segment city) and always 4. Pennants don't matter.
  * With the pig (T&B) a castle is worth **5** (RB-DE3 p.6; JCZ adds +1 per city or castle).
  * The castle is not a city (no knights, no king; RB-DE3 p.6). It also **separates fields** exactly as the small city did (RB-DE3 p.4: "Eine Burg trennt die Wiesen genauso wie die überbaute Kleinstadt.").

## 7. Bazaars

### Z1. When the auction happens

* After the **complete** normal turn with the bazaar tile: placement, follower, scoring, castles (RB-DE3 p.4–5; S-CAR fn 345). No figure may go on the bazaar itself (RB-EN3 p.4, RB-EN1 p.1). The bazaar ground also ends roads (S-CAR fn 344, modified 10/2013), which is a tile-geometry question.
* **The bazaar tile cannot be placed and is discarded:** no bazaar. OC 10/2014 (S-CAR fn 346; WikiCarpedia fn 24).
* **Builder double turn, bazaar in part 1 with the builder triggered.** RB-DE3 p.6: "Wenn du das Basar-Plättchen legst und damit deine Straße oder Stadt mit deinem Baumeister erweiterst, ziehst du **nach Ende der Basar-Runde** dein zweites Plättchen für den Baumeister-Doppelzug und führst diesen aus." RB-EN3 says "completes", a slip for "extends" (WikiCarpedia fn 35). So it goes: auction → bazaar round → your builder part 2. The opposite order (builder part 2 before the auction) comes from a mistranslated 5/2013 clarification (S-CAR fn 347). HiG accepted it in 08/2016 as a valid alternative (WikiCarpedia fn 36), and **JCZ** implements it (`BazaarPhase` runs after `CleanUpTurnPartPhase`). Recommendation: RB-DE3 (Q3).
* **Bazaar drawn as the builder's part-2 tile:** the auction follows that part, i.e. at the end of the turn.
* **Bazaar tile obtained in a bazaar:** no auction. RB-DE3 p.5: "Keine Kettenreaktion: Wird durch ein Basarplättchen ein anderes Basarplättchen versteigert, findet beim Anlegen des zweiten Basars keine Versteigerung statt. Erst nachdem alle versteigerten Plättchen angelegt wurden, kann wieder ein neuer Basar stattfinden."
* **Any bazaar drawn while a bazaar round is running** (e.g. a builder part 2 during the round, or a replacement for an unplaceable bought tile) is **ignored**: OC 2/2021 (WikiCarpedia), "During a bazaar round, if another bazaar tile is placed in a builder-turn, the bazaar will be ignored." JCZ ignores it too: a new auction is flagged only when no bazaar supply is active.

### Z2. The auction, step by step

1. **Reveal** as many tiles from the stack as there are players, face up (RB-DE3 p.5: "Du deckst so viele Plättchen auf, wie Spieler mitspielen."). Every player, the bazaar player included, will end up with exactly one.
2. **The first chooser is the player left of the bazaar player** (RB-DE3 p.5: "Der Spieler, der nach dir an der Reihe ist"). He **picks one tile** and **must open** with a bid of 0 or more points ("er kann auch 0 Punkte bieten").
3. **One bidding round.** Clockwise from the chooser, every **other** player who has no tile yet gets **one** turn: raise the current bid or pass (RB-DE3 p.5: "Reihum ist nun jeder andere Spieler **einmal** an der Reihe. Entweder erhöht er das bestehende Gebot oder er passt."). Raises must be **strictly higher**, so ties are impossible. Bidding does not go round again. **JCZ** `BazaarPhase.nextBidder`: one pass round that skips players who already have a tile; bids must exceed the current price.
4. **The chooser decides** (RB-DE3 p.5), when the high bidder is someone else:
   * **buy**: he takes the tile and pays the high bidder the high bid, **or**
   * **sell**: the high bidder takes the tile and pays the chooser the high bid.

   **If nobody raised**, he takes the tile and pays his own bid to the bank: "Falls der Spieler, der das Plättchen ausgesucht hat, als einziger geboten hat, zieht er die Punkte bei sich ab, ohne dass ein anderer Punkte bekommt." A 0 bid with everyone passing means a free tile. JCZ automatically records a *buy* in that case.
5. **Scores may go negative.** RB-DE3 p.5: "Du darfst auch mehr Punkte bieten, als du bisher gesammelt hast. Gerade zu Beginn des Spiels kommt es daher vor, dass Spieler weniger als 0 Punkte haben." RB-EN3 agrees. (RB-EN1's "A player may not bid more points than he has" was a Z-Man error, corrected in 2018; S-CAR fn 349.) There is no upper limit. The score marker sits below 0 (S-CAR: lay it on its side).
6. **Whoever has received a tile is out** of all further auctions in this bazaar (RB-DE3 p.5). A chooser who **sold** has no tile and stays in.
7. **Next chooser.** RB-DE3 p.5: "Nun ist im Uhrzeigersinn der Nächste an der Reihe, der noch kein Plättchen bekommen hat." Recommended reading: **the next player clockwise after the previous chooser who has no tile yet** (WikiCarpedia fn 26; **JCZ** `nextSelectingPlayer` starts from the previous chooser). RB-EN3 and RB-EN1 say "the next player clockwise from the previous **buyer**", and RGG adds "it could be the same player if he did not buy the tile he chose" (S-CAR fn 350). The readings differ only after a **sale** (Q1).
8. **The last tile is free** for the last player without one (RB-DE3 p.5: "Das letzte Plättchen nimmt sich der letzte verbliebene Spieler umsonst."). There is no auction for it. With 2 players: one auction, then the other player takes the remaining tile.

**Rulebook example (3 players: you, Blue on your left, Black).** Blue picks a tile and bids 2. Black passes, you bid 3, and Blue keeps it, paying you 3. Black picks the next tile and bids 2. You bid 3, and Black sells: you pay Black 3 and take it. Black gets the last tile free. Blue places first, then Black, then you (RB-DE3 p.5).

**Variant without bidding** (official, RB-DE3 p.6, "Basarvariante ohne Bieten"): reveal the same number of tiles. Starting with the next player, each player in turn picks one and **immediately** plays it as a normal turn. Then play continues normally. Worth offering as an option, because many groups prefer it (Carc Central poll, COM).

### Z3. After the auction: the bazaar round

* RB-DE3 p.5: "Nun hat jeder Spieler 1 Plättchen vor sich. Reihum führen alle Spieler mit ihrem ersteigerten Plättchen einen **normalen Zug** mit 1. Ein Plättchen anlegen, 2. Einen Meeple einsetzen und 3. Eine Wertung auslösen aus. **Es beginnt der Spieler nach dem Spieler, der das Basarplättchen gelegt hat.** In dieser Runde zieht natürlich keiner ein Plättchen."
* So every tile is kept until **all** auctions are over. Then, one after another, starting with the player **left of the bazaar player** and ending with the bazaar player, each player takes a **full normal turn** with his tile: bridge, follower, scoring, castles, everything. RB-EN3's "Starting with the first buyer" is a slip (WikiCarpedia fn 28). RB-DE1, RB-EN1 and S-CAR all say "the player after the active player". **JCZ** orders the queue `getPlayersBeginWith(turnPlayer.next)`.
* A normal turn includes a **builder double turn** (OC 2/2010, WikiCarpedia fn 29; S-CAR fn 351). The extra tile is drawn from the stack, and a bazaar on it is ignored (Z1). A **dragon** tile makes the dragon fly at once, and then the round continues (RB-DE3 p.6).
* **Then play resumes normally with the player left of the bazaar player** (RB-EN3 p.5: "Once the auction is complete, play resumes normally starting with the player to your left."). The bazaar round **replaces** one round of normal draws. Sequence: P (bazaar tile) → auction → P+1 … P (bought tiles) → P+1 draws normally. With the builder: … → P (bought tile) → P's builder part 2 → P+1.

### Z4. Special cases

* **Fewer tiles in the stack than players: no auction.** The bazaar is ignored. RB-EN3 p.5: "Skip this step if there are fewer tiles remaining than there are players." The same rule is in the RGG Big Box 3 (S-CAR fn 352). RB-DE3 says nothing. **JCZ**: `tilePack.size() < playersCount → skip`. With exactly as many tiles as players, all are auctioned, and the game ends when the bazaar round is over.
* **A bought tile that cannot be placed** (no legal spot, or the player refuses the only bridge placements, OC 10/2014): no rule. **JCZ** discards it and the player **draws a replacement from the stack**. If the stack is empty, that player's turn is skipped. Points paid are not refunded. Recommendation: follow JCZ (Q9). A bazaar on the replacement is ignored (Z1).
* **A bazaar on a bought tile** never triggers (Z1).
* **Does the bazaar player get a tile? Yes.** There is one tile per player, he can bid, he can be the high bidder (example: "du bietest 3 Punkte"), and he places last.

## 10. Interaction facts found along the way (for the interactions researcher)

From the RB-DE3/EN3 page 6 ("Regeln für das Spiel mit anderen Erweiterungen") unless noted otherwise:

* **I&C:** a large meeple may go on a bridge and onto a castle. Inn and cathedral bonuses count for castle scoring. A BCB inn affects only its printed road (OC 1/2021).
* **T&B:** a builder may go on a bridge. A castle is worth **5** to a farmer with a pig. Bazaar and builder: the bazaar round comes first, then the double turn (RB says "erweiterst"; EN says "completes" in error). Builder double turns inside a bazaar round are allowed (OC 2/2010). Bazaars drawn then are ignored (OC 2/2021).
* **P&D:** the dragon does **not** eat a meeple in a castle, and never eats bridges or castles (P&D EN rulebook). It does eat a meeple **on a bridge**, which stands on the tile (WikiCarpedia). The **fairy** may stand with a meeple in a castle and brings the usual fairy points (RB-DE3 p.6). A late 1st-edition clarification had forbidden this (S-CAR fn 372), but the 3rd-edition text allows it. JCZ moves the fairy into the castle with the meeple. At game end a castle meeple is removed before final scoring, so the fairy gives no +3 then (WikiCarpedia). A dragon tile placed in a bazaar round makes the dragon fly at once.
* **Tower:** a bridge and a tower may share a tile (move the tower aside; OC 11/2013). A tower **cannot** capture a meeple in a castle. It **can** capture a meeple on a bridge (Tower EN rulebook: "May capture … 8. Meeple on bridge"; "May NOT … 8. Meeple in a castle"; bridges and castles are neutral pieces).
* **River:** RB-EN1 p.3 shows bridges spanning rivers. Bridge ends may never sit on river edges (RB-DE3 p.1). Whether bridges may be built during the river phase is not addressed.
* **Abbot:** a bridge may span a garden (RB-DE3 p.2 figure). Gardens trigger castles (COM/JCZ, Q5).

## 11. Edition differences

### 11.1 1st edition (HiG 2010, *Burgen, Brücken und Basare*) vs. 3rd edition (HiG 2017, *Brücken, Burgen und Basare*)

* **Title order** changed (castles first in 2010, bridges first in 2017). The components and per-player counts are unchanged.
* **Tiles:** 12 in both. The 3rd edition has new artwork and one garden tile (JCZ set `:1` has `BB/CFR!`, set `:2` has `BB/CFR!G`). WikiCarpedia notes a misprinted first German 3rd-edition distribution (two tiles missing, two doubled). Tile list researcher.
* **Rule structure:** the 3rd edition hangs everything on the steps 1–3 (bridge in step 1, castle in step 3, bazaar as step "4") and adds a page on other expansions. The 1st edition gave no time for the bridge.
* **Bridges:** the 3rd edition states explicitly "Auf einem Plättchen darf nur 1 Brücke stehen", "hinter eine andere Brücke", and that occupied tiles may be bridged. The 1st edition only had "mehrere Brücken nebeneinander" and "auch schon besetzte Klöster".
* **Castles:** the 3rd edition states explicitly that waiting castle meeples are **removed** before final scoring. The 1st edition said "keine Punkte". The 1st edition put pig and barn (+1 → 5) in the main text; the 3rd edition moved them to page 6. Everything else matches, including "in einem späteren Zug", the semicircle definition, the 6-square area, owner choice and chains.
* **Bazaars:** the procedure is identical. The 3rd edition adds "Du darfst auch mehr Punkte bieten, als du bisher gesammelt hast" and "In dieser Runde zieht natürlich keiner ein Plättchen".
* **Fairy in a castle:** the 1st-edition Big Box 3 allowed it, a later 1st-edition clarification forbade it, and the 3rd edition allows it again.

### 11.2 English translations

* **RB-EN1 (Z-Man 2013)** has these errors: "The Bazaar (12 tiles)" (it is 8); "A player may not bid more points than he has", and if everyone has 0, play the variant (S-CAR fn 349/355); "lord", "fief". Its "two city end segments" was read as allowing triangular segments (S-CAR fn 363). The rest is faithful, including "orthogonally adjacent", bridges over "roads, monasteries, rivers", and "a castle does not score points for a field".
* **RB-EN3 (Z-Man/HiG 2018)** departs from RB-DE3 in these places:
  * "a city consisting of only 2 tiles", instead of two semicircular segments;
  * "in a later turn" is missing;
  * the meeple moving onto the castle, and "a castle with a meeple is not yet completed", are missing;
  * "you decide what order they are scored", instead of the castle owner choosing which feature;
  * "If a castle is scored while neighboring your castle…" instead of the chain sentence;
  * the removal of castle meeples at game end is missing;
  * it adds "Skip this step if there are fewer tiles remaining than there are players";
  * "the next player clockwise from the previous buyer";
  * "Starting with the first buyer";
  * "completes" instead of "extends" (builder);
  * typos: neigboring, choses, inital, auciton.

  It also says "you **must** 4. host a bazaar auction": the bazaar is not optional.

### 11.3 RGG Big Box 3 (via S-CAR)

"It could be the same player if he did not buy the tile he chose" (next chooser). The "too few tiles" rule. Negative scores only "at the very beginning". A scoring guide claims "farms only score completed castles", which S-CAR calls a misprint.

### 11.4 *Castles & Bridges* (HiG 2025, WikiCarpedia "C3.1"): not implemented

This is a different expansion: 16 tiles (one river tile), **no bazaars**, 6 castle tiles, 6 barn tiles and 12 grey bridges in a **general** supply. Its bridges are **neutral**, must be placed whenever a tile's field edge meets an open road, may not touch each other, and are worth **3 points** each when completed. Its castles hold **maidens** worth points in other ways, and it has barns. Recorded here only so that nobody mixes the two up.

## 12. JCloisterZone reference notes (and where JCZ differs from our recommendation)

* **Bridges.** Each player gets `BridgeToken.BRIDGE` × (players < 5 ? 3 : 2). Placement options: the plain tile, then a bridge on the tile, then a bridge on a placed neighbour (`mandatoryBridge`). The player may pass if every placement needs a bridge. An optional bridge appears in the action phase on the **new tile only**, once per turn part, before the meeple; the action phase re-opens after the bridge. *Differs from us:* JCZ has no optional bridge on a neighbour (Q7) and offers only one bridge alternative per position and rotation.
* **Castles.** `CastleToken.CASTLE` uses the same counts. `CastlePhase` comes before scoring and asks, starting with the active player, about cities on the last-placed tile that have exactly 2 `CASTLE_BASE` parts and exactly 1 follower of that player. Conversion moves the followers (and an assigned fairy) onto the `Castle` feature. `scoreCastles` covers all occupied castles not containing the current tile: the vicinity must intersect a completed feature's tiles, the highest value is taken, `ScoreCastle` applies figure bonuses (fairy), the meeple is undeployed, and chains are iterated. Final scoring gives 0. Fields score castles at `pointsPerCity + 1`, and the pig adds 1 per city or castle.
* **Bazaars.** The flag is set when a bazaar tile is placed and no supply is active. `BazaarPhase` comes after the turn-part cleanup, so after the builder part 2 (Q3). It skips if `tilePack.size() < players`. The first chooser is `turnPlayer.next`. The chooser may not pass and bids ≥ 0. Others bid strictly higher or pass, once each. Buy or sell follows, and if the chooser is the high bidder it is an automatic buy paid to nobody. Next chooser: from the previous chooser onwards (Q1). The last tile is free. The queue starts with `turnPlayer.next`. An unplaceable queued tile is replaced by a random draw. Rule option `BAZAAR_NO_AUCTION` is the variant.

## 13. Notes for our engine (no code changed)

* **Groundwork already in place** (commit 816ab7b): `tile.bridge = 0` (N–S) or `1` (E–W) in world directions. `featuresOf(tile)` appends a road feature on the two centre ports and removes those ports from the fields. Edge types on the bridged sides then read `road`. This matches B3/B5. Field joining across a neighbour's road (B5) follows automatically, because the field keeps both outer ports of the bridged side.
* **Legality** (B4): for each position and rotation, collect the mismatching sides.
  * No mismatch: legal with no bridge.
  * Otherwise, if the player has a bridge, try **on the tile**: every mismatch is "new tile field, neighbour road" on the two sides of one axis; both printed end edges are field; each end faces an empty square or a road.
  * Then try **on a neighbour T**: exactly one mismatch, on the side towards T, with the new tile showing road and T showing field; T has no bridge; T's opposite edge is field and faces an empty square.
  * Offer every alternative, and offer *discard* when only bridge placements exist (E1).
* **Optional bridge options** (after placement, if no bridge was used for this tile): the new tile and its orthogonal neighbours, same end tests. Then follower options, including the bridge road on the new tile.
* **Castle eligibility:** a city group with 2 nodes on 2 tiles, each node a 1-side city that is not one of the excluded tiles, completed by this placement and occupied. The tile researcher marks the excluded segments with the city property `"castle_base": false` in `bcb-tiles.json` and notes it is needed on IC/C! and DG/CCc+.p as well. Then ask the owner. On conversion, mark the group as a castle (`{tiles, axis, owner, figure, builtTurnPart}`). Its figure no longer counts as a knight. Fields adjacent to the group score 4 (or 5 with a pig) once.
* **Castle triggers need *unoccupied* completions too.** `completedCloisters` currently reports only monasteries and gardens **with a figure**, and road and city completion detection may likewise skip empty features. Castle scoring needs every road, city, monastery and garden completed by the placement, with its full value (inn and cathedral included), whether or not anyone scores it.
* **Bazaar state** (lockstep-serialisable): `{revealed:[tile], owner:[seat|null], chooser, bidIndex, high:{seat, amount}, phase:'pick'|'bid'|'decide'}`. After it: `bazaarQueue = seats from P+1 round to P` with their tiles; `bazaarActive` blocks new bazaars until the queue (and any builder part 2 inside it) is done. Revealing N tiles is one random draw step; all revealed tiles are public information.

## 14. AI strategy hints

**Bridges** (a scarce resource: 2–3 per game)
* Generate placements **with** bridges. Bridges often turn an unplaceable or poor tile into a scoring one, and late in the game they rescue tiles from discard. Keep at least one in hand for that until the last third of the game.
* Good uses:
  * extend or **complete your own road** across a city, monastery or field tile;
  * lay a bridge on a road-less new tile and put a thief on it, starting a road where none was printed;
  * continue a road into a village or bazaar to complete it;
  * **join two farms**: the field under a bridge joins the fields on both sides of the neighbouring road. Value this as farmer majority and points (3 per completed city, 4 per castle);
  * use a bridge end facing an empty square to force a road edge there, which can spoil a spot an opponent's city or monastery needs (blocking).
* Avoid joining your road to a road where an opponent has more thieves. Avoid opening road ends that only help opponents.

**Castle decision** (owner of a just-completed small city)
* Build the castle only if the expected value of the **first** completion in its 6-square area clearly beats 4 points plus getting the meeple back now. Score each open feature in or touching the area by its value and how likely it is to finish **next**.
* **Opponents will try to "spend" your castle cheaply.** Count how many cheap completions (a 2-tile road, a small city, an almost-finished short road) any player could make there next turn. If there are several, score the 4.
* Strong signs for a castle: a monastery or garden with 6–7 neighbours on an area square; a large city or road you are completing anyway; another waiting castle next to it (chain).
* Weak signs: fewer than about 12–15 tiles left (risk of 0 and losing the meeple), few meeples in reserve, an area full of open small features.
* +1 for farms: a castle is worth 4 instead of 3 to every adjacent farm. Count it if you lead such a farm.

**Playing against castles**
* Complete a **cheap** feature in an opponent's castle area to cash it in low. Don't complete a big feature there unless your own gain is larger than what the castle owner gets.
* A big completion in an area where **you** own a castle pays twice. Steer towards it.

**Bazaar bidding** (v = the best immediate points plus positional value of a tile for a player; f = the expected value of what he will get otherwise)
* **Chooser:** pick the tile with the largest v − f for you, or the tile an opponent wants most if you plan to sell it. After bidding, **buy if v − f > 2·bid**, otherwise sell. Buying costs you the bid and pays it to the bidder; selling does the reverse, a 2·bid swing against that player.
* **Bidder:** never bid more than you would gladly pay for the tile (v − f for you). A raise that forces the chooser to buy is pure profit for you, but he may sell it to you instead (COM analysis, Carc Central 2015).
* The last player gets a free tile: in 3+ player games, waiting can be worth more than a contested tile.
* Opening bids of 0 are normal. Bid and raise in small steps, and remember that negative scores are allowed.
* With 2 players every transfer is directly between the two of you, so weigh bids double.
* A tile that **completes an opponent's** big feature is worth denying: add part of the opponent's gain to its value to you.
* Simple default for the built-in AI: bid ⌊(v − f)/2⌋, capped at 10; chooser rule as above. For the no-bidding variant: just take the highest v.

## 15. Kid rules text (about 250 words)

> **Bridges, Castles & Bazaars**
>
> **Bridges.** You have 3 bridges (2 with 5 or 6 players). Right after placing your tile you may build one, on that tile or on a tile touching one of its sides. It runs straight across, side to opposite side. Both ends must stand on grass, never on a city, road or river; in between it may cross anything. A bridge is road worth 1 point like any road tile, and it never splits a field or a city. A road may even run into grass if a bridge carries it on that same turn. A bridge on your new tile can hold your meeple as a robber.
>
> **Castles.** When a tile finishes a tiny city of two half-round pieces, the knight's owner may build a castle instead of scoring 4. The knight stands on top. In a later turn, the first road, city, monastery, garden or castle finished on one of the castle's 6 neighbour squares (its 2 tiles plus the 2 along each long side) gives the castle owner the same points, even if nobody owned it! Then the knight comes home. Castles still waiting at the end score nothing, but every castle gives farmers 4 points.
>
> **Bazaars.** After a turn with a bazaar tile, turn up one tile per player. Starting on your left, a player picks a tile and bids (0 is fine). Everyone else may raise once. The picker then buys it (paying the top bidder) or sells it (the top bidder pays the picker). Players with a tile drop out, and the last tile is free. Then everyone, starting on your left, plays their tile as a normal turn.
